KEY IDEAS TO UNDERSTAND
MCP
Model Context Protocol over stdio. Four read-only tools. Propose and record_simulated_action are not exposed.
Handoff
Coordinator → runbook specialist → back. Specialist returns citations and stops. Budget: two messages.
Approval gate
Risky writes wait for a person's y/yes. Default is no. Model flags cannot bypass.
Practice 20/20
Offline agent_eval on synthetic fixtures (15 golden + 5 holdout). CI path.
Public 10/26
Ranker-only baseline on public postmortems. Weaker — reported honestly, not mixed with 20/20.
Simulate-only
Approve appends one local log line. Nothing restarts, rolls back, pages, or emails.
